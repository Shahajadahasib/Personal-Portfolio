03 / Projects

Selected work.

Featured · Live in production

Mary Ben's Kitchen

An online ordering platform for a food business in Darwin, NT. One site runs two storefronts, a grocery shop and a restaurant, sharing a single customer account, checkout pipeline and staff dashboard.

  • Grocery catalogue with nested categories, product variants, stock, wishlists and verified reviews
  • Restaurant menu with dietary filters and a modifier builder that reprices the dish live
  • Stripe card payments, distance-based delivery fees and order tracking with email receipts
  • JWT and one-time-code sign-in, rate limiting, background jobs and a documented REST API

My role: I built the initial full-stack platform, then added the security hardening, Stripe checkout and notifications, and kept developing it with collaborators.
